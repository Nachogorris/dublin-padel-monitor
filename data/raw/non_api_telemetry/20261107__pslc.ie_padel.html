Padel @ The PSLC

With five courts in the heart of Portmarnock, Padel @ The PSLC is THE place to play this fast-growing sport.

Padel @ The PSLC serving North County Dublin

Following a major expansion in 2026, the PSLC is now home to five premium padel courts. Located right in the heart of Portmarnock and serving the wider North County Dublin area, our facility offers one of the best value padel experiences around.

All five of our courts are built by top manufacturers and were imported directly from continental Europe to ensure a professional-grade playing experience. Whether you’re a seasoned player or picking up a racket for the very first time, our courts provide a top-tier environment to enjoy the sport.

state of the art panoramic padel courts

We have 3 state of the art panoramic padel courts ideal for players and the public, as it offers a global view of all angles of the court. Our courts are monofilament astro turf grass designed to withstand heavy use and provide a soft, resilient surface for players. This provides excellent ball roll and bounce, making it ideal for sports that require a high degree of ball control.
